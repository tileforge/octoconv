## Tech debt worth deciding before or after close

- **TD-1.** The Helm chart caps uploads at 100 MiB, below the measured IGES, STL and DXF ceilings. It is documented, but a k8s client gets a generic 413 for an input the class supports. This is the only item that changes client-visible behaviour between deployments. A small chart change would close it: per-deployment `MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES` at least the largest CAD ceiling, or a per-format note in the chart values.
- **Everything else** is hardening, measurement tooling or an inherited pattern, and is tracked in pending todos or deferred-items.md.
