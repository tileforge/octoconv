# Quick 261013-0og: codex PR #36 round 4 (a DXF's emptiness is judged by pixels)

## Objective

Codex P2 at 73b98fd, `caddxfpdf.go:1664`: a DXF holding only opaque true-colour white geometry or text renders
onto the white PDF page; `InkOps` counts every non-transparent paint operator without its colour, and the SVG
`Visible` count counts `#ffffff`, so the PDF (and the SVG) route returns a blank-looking file instead of
`cad_empty_result`.

This is round 4 of one class: "is anything visible" decided by attribute heuristics (alpha in round 3; white,
faint and degenerate paint now). No fifth heuristic. The decision moves to the pixel truth the PNG route already
uses: J1 of `cadpixel.go` on a raster of the drawing, the same judge, threshold and background on all three
routes, so svg, pdf and png agree on "empty" by construction.

## Premise check (done before planning)

Ad hoc DXFs through the shipped image (`octoconv-cad-worker:latest`, 27946fc build), the helper and then
`rsvg-convert -b white` into the 2048 letterbox of the render-only SVG, ink counted as any channel below 250:

| DXF | helper | ink pixels at 2048 on white |
|---|---|---|
| LINE, CIRCLE, solid HATCH, SOLID, POINT, true colour white | `ok` | 0 (min channel 255) |
| TEXT and MTEXT, true colour white | `ok` | 0 |
| the white geometry plus one ACI 7 LINE | `ok` | 3594 |
| a collinear SOLID and a zero-area HATCH | `ok` | 0 |
| LINE, HATCH, TEXT at alpha 1/255 (opacity 0.004) | `ok` | 0 (min channel 254) |
| a fully transparent LINE plus an opaque TEXT of U+200B | `ok`, 1 text | 0 |
| an opaque TEXT of U+200B alone | `empty`/`nothing_drawable` (already refused) | - |

Every row with 0 ink converts today on svg and pdf to a blank file. So the finding holds, and it holds for the
whole class the phase-75 verifier listed (white, faint, degenerate fill, invisible character), not only white.

## Decisions fixed before any code

1. **One definition of empty.** A DXF drawing is empty when its validated render-only SVG, drawn by the
   confined `rsvg-convert` into the letterbox of a 2048 x 2048 frame (`CADMaxRenderSize`, the largest frame any
   route renders) over an opaque white page, has no J1 ink pixel: no pixel with a channel below 250. Empty is
   `ErrCADNothingDrawable` + `ErrCADEmptyResult` = `cad_empty_result` on svg, pdf and png (both backgrounds).
2. **Background, per route.**
   - PDF: `-b white`, an explicit white page. Judging against white is exact.
   - Client SVG: it declares its page background as `<rect fill="#ffffff" ... fill-opacity="0.0">`: ezdxf's
     page colour, white, left unpainted (`BackgroundPolicy.OFF`). ezdxf picked every colour for that white page
     (ACI 7 becomes black). The SVG is judged against the white it declares.
   - PNG opaque: the white page itself. PNG transparent: also judged against white. Emptiness is a property of
     the drawing, not of the requested background, so a white-only drawing is `cad_empty_result` as a
     transparent PNG too (it converted before, white strokes on alpha). Recorded in the docs.
3. **Resolution 2048.** Coverage of a stroke or glyph grows with the frame, so the largest frame is the one
   least likely to lose a thin feature; a check at a smaller size could refuse a drawing that a 2048 render
   shows. The render route keeps its own J1 to J3 at the requested size, so its documented limit (features below
   one pixel at a small `size`) is unchanged.
4. **The cheap checks stay as early refusals only.** The helper's `nothing_visible` and Go's `Visible == 0`
   refuse only when no element has opacity above 0, which cannot leave a pixel off white, so they cannot refuse
   a drawing J1 sees ink in; they save the raster for all-transparent drawings and keep the client/render
   agreement check. Proved by a crafted-SVG differential in the probe (every `Visible == 0` case draws 0 ink)
   and by the corpus. The PDF judge's `InkOps` stays, as a check of the PDF artifact: after the raster check
   passed, an inkless PDF means the PDF lost paint the raster drew, which is a renderer fault and stays a
   refusal of that PDF. None of them is the arbiter of "empty" any more.
5. **Mechanics.** `dxfDrawing` always writes the validated render-only SVG to `clean.svg` and, after `Visible`,
   runs the check: `rsvg-convert -b white -w W -h H --page-width 2048 --page-height 2048 --left L --top T -o
   ink.png clean.svg` (the argv and stage of the PNG route and of the sandbox preflight, under the same rsvg
   ruleset), then `JudgeCADDrawingInk` (read the frame, require it opaque and 2048 x 2048, measure with the
   render route's `cadMeasure`, J1 = the shared `cadDrawingJ1`). `ink.png` is removed after judging and joins the
   job-name collision list. A malformed check frame is `ErrCADDrawingFailed` (engine fault, retried), not empty.
6. **Timeouts.** Every DXF job now runs two rsvg stages. `ValidateCADTimeouts` and the evidence test require
   guard + dxf + 2 x rsvg + I/O <= engine (30 + 162 + 282 + 60 = 534 <= 886). The per-stage 141 s holds if the
   measured check raster at the ceilings stays at or under 94 s (141 / 1.5); otherwise re-derive.
7. **Messages.** `ErrCADNothingDrawable`: "the drawing shows nothing on a white page: model space is empty or
   hidden, holds only 3D solids, or everything in it is fully transparent, white, too faint or too thin to
   see". `ErrCADRsvgTimeout`: "the drawing took too long to render or convert to PDF or PNG; ...".
