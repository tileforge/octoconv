<objective>
Close Codex P2 on PR #35 (thread on cmd/cad-worker/helper/cad-mesh:451) as a class, not as one regex. The intake sniffer accepts ASCII STL case-insensitively. Two downstream parsers disagree with it: the numpy stats parser only matches lowercase `vertex`, and VTK's reader fails on a `SOLID`/`Solid` header. The fix has two parts. First, cad-mesh stats parses keywords case-insensitively. Second, stats becomes the only parser of client STL bytes: it writes a normalized binary mesh.stl, and the converter judges and renders that file the same way it already does for STEP and IGES. VTK never sees client bytes.

Purpose: an uppercase or mixed-case ASCII STL that the API admits must render and measure. It must not fail as stl_malformed or render garbage.
Output: the fixed helper and converter with stub-helper tests; a differential script whose evidence covers real-helper behavior, baseline teeth and stats RSS at both ceilings; new cad-liveproof evidence; refreshed sandbox-liveproof evidence; a client-facing note in docs/MEASUREMENTS.md.
