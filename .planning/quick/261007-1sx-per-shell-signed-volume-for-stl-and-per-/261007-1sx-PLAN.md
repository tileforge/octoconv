<objective>
Close Codex P2 round 2 on PR #35 (thread on cmd/cad-worker/helper/cad-mesh:605) as a class. Today `stats_mass` adds every triangle's signed volume. A watertight STL whose disjoint shells have opposite winding therefore publishes the difference of their volumes as a positive number. `solid_summary` has the same defect: one reversed STEP/IGES solid subtracts from the others. A probe during planning confirmed this in the image: box 10 plus reversed box 5 gives `(2, 2, 874.99..., 749.99..., '')`.

The fix computes signed volume per shell for STL and checks the sign per solid for STEP/IGES. It never publishes a sum across signs. A mixed-sign STL gets a new closed-vocabulary null reason `stl_ambiguous_shells`. This is honest about a hollow part (outer shell outward, cavity inward): the document reports null, never a wrong number.

Purpose: a measurement is either the physical volume or a null with a documented reason.
Output: the vocabulary commit, the helper fix, an extended differential script and liveproof row, and fresh 74-08 evidence.
