## Tech Debt

### Phase 52 — Quota Enforcement (7 items + 1 accepted deviation)

The heaviest concentration, and the most consequential is **WR-01**: making the daily cap real
(the fix that closed the phase's one blocker) necessarily made in-flight work charge the daily
budget. A job that never reaches a terminal state therefore charges that budget on every subsequent
UTC day, indefinitely. This self-heals in a correctly operated deployment — the reconciler exhausts
retries and calls `MarkFailed` — but that makes quota availability newly dependent on
`cmd/webhook-worker`'s health, and there is no operator-side remedy in `cmd/manage-clients` (only
raw SQL; `DELETE /v1/jobs/{id}` is owner-gated). Neither the coupling nor the remedy gap is
documented. Phase 52's own verifier reached this verdict independently and classified it non-blocking;
this audit agrees, and flags it as the single highest-value follow-up in the milestone.

Alongside it, `README.md`'s quota section still self-contradicts (line 242 promises enforcement
"before the upload touches storage"; line 294 explains the pre-upload gate is unlocked by design) —
a reader sizing a quota off the opening line would draw the wrong conclusion.

### Phase 53 — Operator Visibility (4 items)

All informational: unbounded all-clients response, mistyped-engine rows reported as enforceable
budget, an unenforced non-nil contract, plus one environment issue (the shared dev MinIO volume at
100% disk breaking `internal/storage` tests) that is infra housekeeping, not code.

### Phase 54 — Capacity Floor (4 items, 2 of them accepted overrides)

CI hygiene only. The two overrides (tag-pinned `azure/setup-helm`, no `permissions:` on pre-existing
jobs) carry recorded rationale in the phase's verification frontmatter.

### Phase 55 — Live Gate (1 item)

An unflagged deviation, not a functional gap: the harness proves the cross-replica claim with
aggregate per-pod admit/reject counts, where the plan's own acceptance criteria asked for a
transcript line naming a specific admitted and a specific rejected request-id on different pods.
Worth noting because a future reader will otherwise go looking in the transcript for a line that
isn't there.

### Cross-phase (1 item)

`PurgeUsage` uses the Go wall clock where the rest of this milestone deliberately moved date
computation onto Postgres's clock. Harmless at 400 days; inconsistent with the discipline the
milestone itself established.
