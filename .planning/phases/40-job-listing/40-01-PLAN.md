<objective>
Build the client-scoped, keyset-paginated read layer for job listing entirely inside `internal/jobs`: a `JobSummary` row type, a `ListFilter`, an opaque cursor codec, and `Repo.List` — one SQL query per page that hard-scopes `client_id` at the SQL layer (JLIST-01), pages by `(created_at DESC, id DESC)` keyset (JLIST-02), and selects everything an item needs directly from the `jobs` row (no N+1).

Purpose: The SQL `WHERE client_id = $1` here is the load-bearing cross-client-isolation guarantee — it must be proven by a two-client DB test, never inferred. The keyset ordering is what keeps pages stable under this table's continuous inserts.
Output: `internal/jobs/{cursor.go, cursor_test.go, list_test.go}` (new), extended `jobs.go` and `repo.go`.
</objective>
