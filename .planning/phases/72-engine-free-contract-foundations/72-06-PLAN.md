<objective>
Wire the four rejection classes into both endpoints: 422 codes in job creation, sentinel formats in
inspect, the reason bindings and their documentation, and one table test that posts every fixture to
both endpoints and proves their verdicts agree.

Purpose: this is where CADIN-01..03 become observable over HTTP. The decision already exists (72-04);
this plan surfaces it twice through one function so the two endpoints cannot disagree, and keeps the
frozen inspect goldens untouched by reporting rejections as sentinels (orchestrator decision 1).

Output: create-path rejection switch, inspect sentinels and checks, RejectBindings plus FINDINGS rows,
the InspectReport.format contract text, and the agreement tests.
</objective>
