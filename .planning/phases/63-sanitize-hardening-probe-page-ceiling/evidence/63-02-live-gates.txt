### What these three runs do NOT prove, stated so nobody reads more into them

1. The under-ceiling case went through the ABSTAIN path, not the confident one.
   threat_all_classes.pdf carries a hex-escaped PDF name, so the API's bounded
   walk declines to claim a count (reason=pdf_name_escape above) and admits on
   that basis. So this run proves "admitted and sanitized end to end" -- which
   is the P1 regression on the wire -- and NOT "counted as 1 page and accepted".
   The confident-accept path is covered elsewhere: the poppler differential
   table answers on 8 committed PDFs with no disagreement, and
   TestBothProbeGatesResolveOneBound pins accept-at-exactly-max.

2. The worker log carries no too_many_pages line, because the worker does not
   log per-job classifications. The terminal refusal is asserted where the
   client actually sees it -- GET /v1/jobs/{id} reporting status=failed with
   error_code=too_many_pages -- which is the contract, not the log line.

Both gates fired on the SAME 201-page document in this run, by two different
routes: refused at admission when the count was legible (over.pdf), and refused
terminally in the worker when an appended /ObjStm made it illegible to the cheap
walk (blind.pdf). That pair is D-04's whole design, observed rather than argued.
