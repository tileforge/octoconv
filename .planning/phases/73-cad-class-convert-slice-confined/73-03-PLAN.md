Re-measure the two rulesets on the image that ships, record them once, and hold the record equal to the code.

Purpose: CADW-02 asks for a MEASURED ruleset per stage. The phase-71 sets were measured on a different image
(VTK, OSMesa, fonts, another helper path) with a different argv; plan 73-02 seeded the Go vars from that
candidate. This plan measures the production engine stage with the production helpers and argv, replaces
the seeds with what the removal table proves, writes the SANDBOX-PATHS section, and extends the crosscheck so
document and code cannot drift.

Output: scripts/cad-worker-sandbox-measure.sh, evidence/73-03-landlock.txt, the `## cad` section, the
measured Go vars and their pins, conf05-crosscheck support for cad.
</objective>
