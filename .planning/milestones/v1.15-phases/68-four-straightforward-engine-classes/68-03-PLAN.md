pdftoppm, pdfinfo, pdftotext — goes through the job's engineExec (plan 01) under the set

pdfinfo/pdftotext guards in ocrguards.go are package functions but are called only from
inside OCRConverter (ocrPDFGuardStage, ocrPDFPage); nothing outside the converter execs an
OCR binary. They still take the engineExec explicitly — "inside the converter" is not the
same as "under the ruleset".

Purpose: CONF-01/02/04/05 for ocr — including the PDF path the first measurement missed.
Output: confined OCR converter, preflight, worker wiring, chart extraEnv, live gate.
