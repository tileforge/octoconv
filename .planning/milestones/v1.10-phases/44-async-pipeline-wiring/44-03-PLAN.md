Wire OCR into the live worker lifecycle: `HandleOCRConvert` (a clone of `HandleAVConvert` — download → ConvertWithProgress → upload → MarkDone, with the exact progressSink + cancel wiring), the stage-aware `isOCRTerminal` classifier (D-04), the `ocrFailureCode` mapping, `cmd/ocr-worker` (a clone of `cmd/av-worker` minus the AV-specific env), and reconciler routing for `engine='ocr'`.
