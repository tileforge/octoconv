2. scripts/cad-dxf-bounds.sh writes the RULE lines first, verbatim from this action, then measures. Rows: ladder M at n in 60000, 100000, 120000, 135000, 150000 and 170031 cells of the 5-entity block; ceiling rows R at 100 and 118 MiB replicates of public/uncommon.dxf. Per row, each in its own container: guard (caps set ten times above the row so it parses fully; wall and peak only), cad-dxf confined (--max-drawn-entities above the row's expansion), cad-svg-check re-serializing draw.svg to clean.svg (GO_SIDE), rsvg-convert to PDF, cad-pdf-check on it (GO_SIDE), rsvg-convert to PNG 2048 opaque and to PNG 2048 transparent with the letterbox from cad-pixel-judge --print-letterbox, cad-pixel-judge --drawing on both (GO_SIDE). BOUND_ROW per engine stage with row, n or bytes, sha256, stage, rep, rc, wall_s, child_rss_mib, cg_peak_mib, oom_kill, peak_mib, pct_of_budget, and output bytes (svg, pdf, png). Every rc must be 0 and every judge ok, else STOP. Rep 1 for every row; rep 2 for the rows that set a DERIVED value. Peak and wall of a row and stage are the maxima over its reps. Rows are buffered and the BOUND_ROW lines written after the derivation, so each carries admitted=0|1.
   The rule (pre-registered; R-7, R-5, R-8):
   a. LIMIT = 0.70 x 4096 - 24.9 = 2842.3 MiB (the budget itself, corrected R-7); row peak = max over guard, dxf and the three rsvg runs; pct_of_budget = row peak / 2842.3 on every row.
   b. cap_inserts = the largest ladder n whose row peak and every smaller rung's row peak are at most LIMIT, clamped to 170031; if 60000 is already over, STOP (reason smallest_rung_over_limit).
   c. cap_entities = 5 x cap_inserts (R-8's derivation of 850155 from the 5-entity series, carried to the new cap).
   d. ceiling = 118 MiB (123731968 bytes) if the 118 row peak is at most LIMIT, else 100 MiB if the 100 row is, else STOP.
   e. Check rows after b-d: B = dxf-minsert with a 50-entity block at floor(cap_entities / 50) cells (at most cap_inserts); C = dxf-combo on the replicate at the derived ceiling with min(cap_inserts - its expanded inserts, floor((cap_entities - its expanded entities) / 5)) cells, the replicate's counts taken from the guard. The guard with the derived caps must say ok for both; both row peaks must be at most LIMIT; else STOP (reason check_row_over_limit or check_row_not_admitted).
   f. Admitted rows = the ladder rungs up to cap_inserts, the ceiling rows up to the ceiling, B and C. dxf_timeout_s = ceil(1.5 x max cad-dxf wall over admitted rows); rsvg_timeout_s = ceil(1.5 x max rsvg wall over admitted rows and all three variants); the guard keeps 30 s only if 1.5 x its max wall is at most 30, else STOP; 30 + dxf_timeout_s + rsvg_timeout_s + 60 must be at most 886, else STOP.
   g. svg_max_bytes and pdf_max_bytes = ceil(1.5 x the max output bytes over admitted rows) rounded up to a whole MiB; every PNG at most 96 MiB (cadPixelMaxBytes) else STOP.
   h. Go side: record wall and peak of the three Go steps per row; the sum of the Go walls on the slowest admitted row must leave the attempt inside 886 s after the stage timeouts and 60 s I/O, else STOP; a Go peak above 24.9 MiB is a `GAP,key=go_worker_rss_dxf,...` for phase 76, not a stop.
   DERIVED lines carry the values with their arithmetic: `DERIVED,key=<key>,value=<integer>,derivation=...` (cad_dxf_peak_limit_mib carries 2842.3). smoke mode runs the same steps on n in 100 and 1000 and the 2 MiB replicate into a temporary file with the RULE lines and a SUMMARY, and derives nothing.
