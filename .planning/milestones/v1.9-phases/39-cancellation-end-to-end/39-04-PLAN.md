<objective>
Prove the two hardest guarantees of this phase: non-resurrection (CNCL-04) and real
process death (CNCL-03 live). Two concerns:
  1. Regression tests that pin the Postgres-authoritative backstop: FindStale excludes
     canceled (D-10), the guarded-transition allow-list rejects MarkActive/MarkDone/MarkFailed
     after Cancel (D-05), and FindWebhookGaps never sweeps canceled (D-10). These are the
     mechanisms that make cancel terminal regardless of whether the pub/sub signal ever
     arrived — independent of the handler's context.Canceled classification (which the
     asynq#987 race can bypass).
  2. An operator-run live proof: submit a long real av transcode, cancel it mid-flight, and
     assert via the process table that the real ffmpeg child is genuinely dead — mirroring the
     Phase 9 zombie-reaping precedent. `go test -race` alone is explicitly insufficient here.

Purpose: Guarantee a canceled job is never resurrected and that cancel kills the real process.
Output: repo/reconciler regression tests + scripts/cancel-liveproof.sh + an operator verification.

Locked decisions honored: D-05 (allow-list is the authoritative backstop), D-10 (explicit
FindStale + webhook-gap regression), CNCL-03 live-proof shape (process-table assertion, not
just the DB row). This plan adds NO new production behavior — it verifies Plans 01/03.
</objective>
