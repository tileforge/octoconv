A NOTE ON THE ONE THING THAT DID MOVE, AND WHY IT IS NOT A CHANGE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A first pass compared `helm template --show-only templates/scaledobject-
probe.yaml` before and after and reported probe as CHANGED -- by one trailing
blank line. It is a document separator between concatenated manifests: probe's
block was last in the render before, and is now followed by tectonic's. The
per-object comparison above is what settles it, and it is the reason the
comparison was redone at the YAML-document level instead of on text: a text
diff cannot tell a manifest change from a concatenation artifact, and reporting
either one as the other would be wrong in a different direction.

The vacuity guard in that comparison earned its place immediately: yq's output
includes `---` separators, which the first version of the loop happily treated
as two more object names. They printed VACUOUS rather than passing silently.
