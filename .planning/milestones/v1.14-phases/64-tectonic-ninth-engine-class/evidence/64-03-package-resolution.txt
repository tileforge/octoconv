WHAT THIS CHANGED
-----------------
64-03 as planned gates admission on convert.TeXPackages -- the DECLARED
eighteen. All twelve RESOLVES rows above are outside that set. So the planned
gate would have refused, with "package not supported", twelve documents this
service typesets correctly today. Five of the twelve -- fontenc, inputenc,
lmodern, microtype, textcomp -- are in pandoc's DEFAULT LaTeX template, and
one of them (fontenc) is in the paper 64-04 ran end to end and verified.

That is 63-02's defect exactly: an admission gate that refuses valid work,
which was built, measured, and REMOVED, with PROBE-01 reworded around the
finding.

So the gate refuses what the ENGINE CANNOT RESOLVE, derived from the bundle
that ships (608 .sty files, written by scripts/tectonic-bundle-build.sh in the
same run that writes MANIFEST.txt, embedded as internal/convert/
texresolvable.txt). convert.TeXPackages stays what it always was: the
documented promise, held equal to the bundle and to docs/TYPESETTING.md by
64-01's three-way test.

TEX-03's wording ("outside the declared set") is reworded to match the goal it
states in its own next clause -- a clear refusal NAMING the package instead of
an unexplained engine failure. Decision confirmed with the operator before any
of it was built.

The negative half is why the gate is still a gate: pgfplots, siunitx, minted,
csquotes and mathtools are refused at admission, before the upload, instead of
after a queue round trip and a compile.
